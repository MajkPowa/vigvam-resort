Frequently asked questions

How do I find available dates?

Select your dates and guests. The form prepares an email for reception to confirm availability and price.

Do the barrel cabins have bathrooms?

Yes. Both the wooden barrel cabin and the larger barrel suite have their own shower and toilet.

Can we bring a dog?

The dog supplement is CZK 250 per night. Please mention your dog when enquiring.

How do I plan a wedding reception?

Use the wedding planner to choose a date, guest count, venue and table layout. Include your ideas for the meal, decorations and accommodation.

Can I request just bowling or the spa?

Open the individual service page and enter your preferred day, time and group size. Reception will check available sessions.
