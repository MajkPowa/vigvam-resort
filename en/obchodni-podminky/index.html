Booking conditions

Reservations and payments

An enquiry records what you would like to book. The resort or booking provider will supply the confirmed dates, total cost and payment arrangements before you complete the booking.

Fees and cancellation

The published stay rates exclude the local charge of CZK 25 per person per day.

The dog supplement is CZK 250 per night. Please mention your dog when enquiring.

Cancellation 6 to 14 days before arrival costs 50% of the stay. From four days before arrival to arrival day the charge is 100%. The published bands overlap on day five. Reception can confirm the terms for that day before you book.
